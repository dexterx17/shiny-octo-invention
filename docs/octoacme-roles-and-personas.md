# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure user-centric design practices are embedded throughout the product lifecycle. They validate workflows through usability testing, create wireframes and prototypes, and collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to refine requirements that meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces, interactions, and information architecture
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation fidelity
- Validate designs through user feedback and iterative testing

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support adoption
- Ensure design consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Collaboration with Developers during sprint planning and implementation
- Usability test findings shared with Product and QA teams
- Design system and pattern library updates

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coach the team on agile principles and practices. They remove impediments, foster continuous improvement, and help the team maintain sustainable velocity while following agile best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and remove blockers
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization
- Protect the team from external disruptions and scope changes
- Track and communicate team velocity and capacity
- Foster a culture of transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Enable team to deliver consistently and predictably
- Maximize team efficiency and morale
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and agile ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Coordination with Project Managers on schedules and dependencies
- Impediment escalation to leadership when needed

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate all aspects of release cycles, ensuring features move smoothly from development to production. They manage release schedules, coordinate across teams, enforce readiness criteria, and oversee deployment activities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and timelines
- Manage release readiness checklists and gate criteria
- Coordinate across Product, Engineering, QA, and Operations teams
- Track release progress and communicate status to stakeholders
- Manage code freeze periods and deployment windows
- Ensure release documentation and runbooks are complete
- Coordinate rollback procedures when necessary

### Goals
- Deliver releases on time with minimal production issues
- Ensure smooth coordination across all release stakeholders
- Maintain clear communication and transparency throughout release cycles

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Engineering and Product
- Go/no-go decisions with stakeholders before deployment
- Release status updates to leadership and support teams
- Post-release retrospectives and lessons learned

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain high-quality documentation that helps users, developers, and stakeholders understand and effectively use the product. They ensure consistency, clarity, and accuracy across all technical content.

### Responsibilities
- Write and maintain user-facing documentation (guides, FAQs, tutorials)
- Create and update API documentation and developer guides
- Document internal processes, runbooks, and operational procedures
- Collaborate with Product Managers to ensure accuracy and completeness
- Work with Developers to understand technical implementation details
- Maintain documentation standards and style guides
- Review and edit content created by other team members

### Goals
- Deliver clear, accurate, and comprehensive documentation
- Reduce support burden through self-service resources
- Ensure documentation stays current with product changes

### Typical Communication
- Documentation review sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Content planning discussions aligned with release cycles
- Feedback loops with support teams on common user questions
- Style guide and template updates shared with the team

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads oversee security risk assessments, reviews, and compliance throughout the project lifecycle. They define security standards, coordinate vulnerability responses, and ensure security best practices are embedded in development and deployment processes.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security risk assessments and threat modeling
- Review code and architecture for security vulnerabilities
- Define and enforce security standards and compliance requirements
- Coordinate security incident response and remediation
- Oversee security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- Provide security training and guidance to development teams
- Maintain security documentation and risk registers

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and incidents
- Ensure compliance with security policies and regulations
- Build security awareness and best practices across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions during design and code review
- Risk register updates with Project Managers
- Incident response coordination with on-call and operations
- Security training and knowledge sharing with Developers and QA

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals ensure software quality by validating features against acceptance criteria, identifying defects, and verifying that releases meet quality standards. They work closely with developers and product managers to maintain high quality throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Create and execute test plans and test cases
- Perform manual and automated testing (functional, integration, regression)
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Identify, document, and track defects
- Collaborate with developers on bug reproduction and fixes
- Participate in test automation and CI/CD quality gates
- Conduct usability testing with UX Designer for user-facing features

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Catch issues early in the development cycle
- Improve test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups to report testing status and blockers
- Bug reports and defect tracking
- Test plan reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Go/no-go recommendations before releases
